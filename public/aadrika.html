Aadrika

Aadrika is marketing software for businesses. It helps a small team plan campaigns, write and design posts, emails and web pages, publish them to the platforms the business already uses, and see what worked, all in one place.

Sign in to Aadrika

How it works

  • A person approves every step. Aadrika's AI drafts content and suggests next steps, but nothing is published, sent or spent until someone on your team approves it.
  • Your own accounts. You connect your existing accounts with the platform's official sign-in. Aadrika never asks for or stores your passwords, and you can disconnect at any time.
  • Your brand. Your logo, colours, tone and approved facts are kept in one library, so everything Aadrika drafts stays on brand.

Why Aadrika asks for access to Google

If you choose Connect Google, Aadrika asks only for the access these features need:

Google serviceWhat you see in Aadrika
Google Search Console (read-only)Which of your pages people find in Google search, and which ones to improve
Google Analytics (read-only)Visits and results for your website and campaigns, in your results page
YouTube and YouTube Analytics (read-only)Views and engagement for your channel's videos

Aadrika uses Google data only to show you these results. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models. Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Full details are in our privacy policy.
