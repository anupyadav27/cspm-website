# Cloud service icons: sources and terms

These are the providers' own published icons. They were downloaded on 27 Nov 2026 and are used unmodified, one per service, on the solution pages' coverage map. They are on trial: the owner will review the design and keep them, replace them with licensed or paid icons, or remove them.

| Folder | Source | Terms to check before keeping |
|---|---|---|
| `aws/` | AWS Architecture Icons, package 07/31/2026 (aws.amazon.com/architecture/icons) | AWS allows customers and partners to use the icons in architecture diagrams. Confirm that use on a marketing page is covered, or ask AWS Partner marketing. |
| `azure/` | Azure Public Service Icons V25 (learn.microsoft.com/azure/architecture/icons) | Microsoft permits use in architecture diagrams, training materials and documentation, unmodified. Confirm the marketing-page use. |
| `gcp/` | Google Cloud icons: core products pack and legacy pack (cloud.google.com/icons) | Google's icon and brand guidelines apply. Confirm the marketing-page use. |
| `kubernetes/` | Kubernetes community icons (github.com/kubernetes/community/tree/master/icons) | Apache-2.0 or CC-BY-4.0. The Kubernetes logo itself is a Linux Foundation trademark and is not used here. |

The service names are the providers' trademarks. Using them describes what Onam scans; it does not imply a partnership or endorsement.

OCI, Alibaba Cloud and IBM Cloud have no SVG pack in this folder yet. Their pages fall back to the category icons.
