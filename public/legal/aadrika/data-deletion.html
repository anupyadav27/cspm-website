Aadrika — How to delete your data

Remove Aadrika's access to Facebook or Instagram

  1. In Aadrika, open Settings → Connections and choose Disconnect next to Facebook or Instagram. Aadrika removes the stored access and can no longer post, read or run ads for that account.
  2. You can also remove the app from Facebook: go to Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations (or Apps and websites), find Aadrika, and choose Remove.

Delete the data Aadrika holds

Email privacy@onamsecurity.com from the address on your account, with the subject "Delete my data", and say whether you want:

  • only the data that came from Facebook and Instagram (page and post details, ad results), or
  • your whole Aadrika account and everything in it.

We confirm within 3 working days and finish the deletion within 30 days. You will get a confirmation code you can use to check its status with us.

What we may keep

Records we must keep by law, such as invoices, and the "do not contact" list that stops us contacting someone who asked us not to. These hold the minimum needed and are not used for anything else.
