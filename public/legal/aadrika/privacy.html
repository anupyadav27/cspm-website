This policy explains what information Aadrika handles, why, and the choices you have.

1. Who this policy covers

  • Customers: businesses and the people who sign up for and use Aadrika.
  • Our customers' audiences: people whose details a customer brings into Aadrika, such as email subscribers, contacts and leads. For this information the customer is the controller and we are its processor. We act only on the customer's instructions.
  • Visitors to Aadrika's website.

3. AI processing

Aadrika uses AI models to draft and check content. The prompts and the material needed for a draft go to the model providers we use (currently Anthropic, DeepSeek and Google) through one controlled gateway. We send customer data only to providers whose business terms do not allow them to train their models on it. AI drafts are proposals: nothing is published, sent or spent without approval by a person on your team.

4. Where data is kept and who processes it

Data is stored with our cloud provider in India by default. Service providers who process data for us: our cloud host, Amazon Web Services (email delivery through Amazon SES), the AI model providers above, and the platforms you connect. Each one gets only what it needs for its task.

5. How long we keep it

  • Account data: while your account is active, and for 30 days after it is closed, then deleted.
  • Audit and security records: 1 year, because the law may require us to keep them.
  • Unsubscribe and "do not contact" records: kept for as long as needed to make sure we never contact that person again.

6. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Each customer's data is kept separate from every other customer's. Access is limited to the people who need it and is logged. Connected-account tokens are held in a secrets store, never in plain files.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 and the EU or UK GDPR, you can ask to see, correct, export or delete your personal information, or withdraw consent. To ask, email privacy@onamsecurity.com. We answer within 30 days. If you are a contact of one of our customers, we will pass your request to that customer and help them answer it.

How to delete data that came from Facebook or Instagram is explained in our data deletion instructions.

8. Children

Aadrika is a business service and is not meant for anyone under 18.

9. Changes

We will post changes here and email account owners about any material change before it takes effect.
