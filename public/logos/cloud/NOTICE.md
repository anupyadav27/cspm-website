Each provider's own logo, as an SVG downloaded unmodified from Wikimedia Commons on 27 Nov 2026. They are on trial: the owner will review the look and then keep them, replace them with versions from each provider's brand or partner kit, or remove them.

All are trademarks of their owners. They are used to say which clouds Onam connects to, not to claim a partnership or endorsement. Before keeping them, check each provider's trademark guidelines. AWS and Microsoft, for example, generally ask non-partners to use their names in text rather than their logos. The partner or marketplace programmes give approved logo kits.
